1. Exact file and line numbers of Dispatcher invalidation bugs.
2. Legacy foundation component debt catalogued and scored.
3. Actionable remediation roadmap with verified before/after benchmarks.
4. A 45-minute live debrief with an experienced AEM Tech Lead.
